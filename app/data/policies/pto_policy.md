# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

## Overview
This policy outlines the Paid Time Off (PTO) benefits for all full-time employees.

## PTO Allowance
- **New employees**: 15 days PTO per year (accrued monthly)
- **After 3 years**: 20 days PTO per year
- **After 5 years**: 25 days PTO per year

## Accrual and Usage
- PTO accrues at the rate of 1.25 days per month for new employees
- Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days to the next year
- PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance for planned leave
- Emergency leave requires manager notification within 24 hours

## Holidays
The following company holidays are observed and do not count against PTO:
- New Year's Day
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day
- Labor Day
- Thanksgiving (2 days)
- Christmas Eve & Christmas Day

## Sick Leave
- Separate from PTO: 10 sick days per year
- Can be used for personal illness or caring for immediate family members
- Doctor's note required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days

## Request Process
1. Submit request through the HR portal
2. Manager approval required
3. HR will confirm within 5 business days

# Leave of Absence Policy

## Types of Leave

### Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)
- 12 weeks unpaid leave per year for:
  - Birth/adoption of child
  - Serious health condition of employee or immediate family
  - Qualifying military exigency
- Must have worked 12 months, 1,250 hours
- Maintains health benefits during leave
- Requires certification from healthcare provider

### Personal Leave
- Up to 4 weeks unpaid leave per year
- Requires manager approval
- Must submit 30 days in advance
- Benefits can be continued at employee cost

### Parental Leave
- 16 weeks paid leave for primary caregivers
- 8 weeks paid leave for secondary caregivers
- Must be taken within 12 months of birth/adoption
- Can be combined with FMLA

### Bereavement Leave
- Immediate family: 5 days paid
- Extended family: 3 days paid
- Travel time may be granted

### Jury Duty
- Paid leave for jury service
- Must provide court notice
- Any jury fee can be kept by employee

### Voting Time
- Paid time to vote (up to 2 hours)
- Must vote before or after work hours if possible

## Request Process
1. Submit written request to manager
2. Provide documentation as required
3. HR will confirm within 5 business days
4. Coordinate with team for coverage

## Returning to Work
- Must provide medical clearance if applicable
- Same or equivalent position guaranteed
- Contact HR 1 week before return date

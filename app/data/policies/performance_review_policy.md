# Performance Review Policy

## Review Cycle
- Annual performance review: January each year
- Mid-year check-in: July
- New employees: review after 6 months, then annually
- Reviews conducted by direct manager

## Review Components

### Goal Achievement (40%)
- Goals set at beginning of review period
- SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- Documentation of achievements throughout year
- Self-assessment required before review

### Core Competencies (30%)
- Communication
- Teamwork
- Problem-solving
- Innovation
- Leadership (for managers)

### Values Alignment (30%)
- Adherence to company values
- Professional conduct
- Collaboration
- Customer focus

## Rating Scale
| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 5 - Exceptional | Consistently exceeds expectations |
| 4 - Exceeds | Frequently exceeds expectations |
| 3 - Meets | Consistently meets expectations |
| 2 - Developing | Partially meets expectations |
| 1 - Unsatisfactory | Does not meet expectations |

## Merit Increases
- Based on performance rating and company performance
- Range: 0% to 8% salary increase
- Effective April 1 each year

## Performance Improvement
- Written improvement plan for ratings of 1 or 2
- 90-day improvement period
- Regular check-ins with manager
- HR involvement required

## Career Development
- Discuss career goals during review
- Create development plan
- Internal mobility opportunities
- Mentorship program available

# Expense Reimbursement Policy

## Eligible Expenses
- Business travel (airfare, hotel, ground transportation)
- Meals with clients (up to $75 per person)
- Professional development (conferences, courses, certifications)
- Office supplies and equipment
- Internet and phone (for remote employees, up to $50/month)

## Reimbursement Limits
| Expense Type | Maximum |
|--------------|---------|
| Airfare | $500 domestic, $1200 international |
| Hotel | $200/night domestic, $350/night international |
| Meals | $75/person client, $50/day individual |
| Car rental | $50/day economy |
| Parking | $30/day |

## Required Documentation
- Original itemized receipts required
- Business purpose must be documented
- Include meeting agenda or event confirmation
- Submit within 30 days of expense

## Approval Process
1. Submit expense report through Concur system
2. Attach all receipts
3. Manager approval required for expenses over $100
4. Finance processes within 10 business days
5. Reimbursement via direct deposit

## Non-Reimbursable Items
- Personal entertainment
- Alcoholic beverages (except client dinners with approval)
- Upgrades (flight, hotel room)
- Spouse/partner travel
- Traffic violations
- Personal purchases

## Travel Advances
- Available for trips over $500
- Request 2 weeks in advance
- Advance limited to 80% of estimated costs
- Final reconciliation within 30 days of trip

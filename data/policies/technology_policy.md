# Technology Acceptable Use Policy

## Company Equipment
- Computers and devices are company property
- Personal use permitted if minimal and appropriate
- No unauthorized software installations
- Regular updates and security patches required
- Return all equipment upon termination

## Email and Communication
- Company email for business communications only
- No mass personal emails or chain letters
- Be cautious of phishing attempts
- Report suspicious emails to IT
- No professional conduct in emails

## Internet Usage
- Business-related browsing permitted
- No excessive personal browsing
- No illegal content access
- No gambling or gaming sites
- Social media limited to break times

## Software and Applications
- Only approved software from company catalog
- No unapproved cloud storage services
- Report software bugs to IT
- Free trials require IT approval

## Mobile Devices
- Company mobile devices for business use
- Enable remote wipe for lost devices
- Password protected
- Company email only on personal devices with MDM

## Monitoring
- Company reserves right to monitor:
  - Email and instant messages
  - Internet usage
  - File transfers
  - Computer activity
- No expectation of privacy on company systems

## Violations
- First offense: verbal warning
- Second offense: written warning
- Third offense: termination
- Serious violations: immediate termination

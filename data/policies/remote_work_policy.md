# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility
All full-time employees may request to work remotely after completing their probationary period (90 days).

## Hybrid Work Schedule
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week
- Minimum 2 days must be in-office for team collaboration
- Core hours: 10 AM - 3 PM in your local timezone

## Equipment and Setup
- Company provides: laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse
- Employees are responsible for:
  - Reliable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps)
  - Quiet workspace for video calls
  - Adequate lighting for video conferences

## Communication Requirements
- Must be available on Slack during core hours
- Response time: within 30 minutes during work hours
- Camera on for team meetings
- Update calendar with availability

## Security
- Use company VPN when accessing internal systems
- Multi-factor authentication required for all work accounts
- No public WiFi for sensitive work
- Physical security: lock screen when away

## Remote Work Agreement
- Formal agreement required with manager and HR
- Performance reviews consider remote work effectiveness
- May be revoked if performance declines

# Employee Benefits Overview

## Health Insurance
- **Medical**: Three plan options (PPO, HMO, HDHP)
- Company pays 80% of premiums
- Coverage begins first of month after hire date
- Dependents can be added within 30 days
- Open enrollment: November each year

### Plan Details
- PPO: $50 copay, $500 deductible
- HMO: $25 copay, $250 deductible (network required)
- HDHP: 20% coinsurance, $1500 deductible (HSA eligible)

## Dental Insurance
- Delta Dental PPO
- 100% preventive care covered
- Basic: 80% covered
- Major: 50% covered
- Annual maximum: $2000 per person

## Vision Insurance
- VSP Choice Plan
- Annual eye exam: $10 copay
- Frames: $150 allowance every 2 years
- Contact lenses: $150 allowance annually

## Life and Disability
- **Life insurance**: 2x salary (up to $500k)
- **Short-term disability**: 60% salary for up to 12 weeks
- **Long-term disability**: 60% salary to age 65

## Retirement Benefits
- 401(k) plan with 4% company match
- Immediate vesting for company match
- Traditional and Roth options
- Managed by Fidelity

## Additional Benefits
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
- Wellness program ($500 annual)
- Tuition reimbursement ($5000/year)
- Professional development budget ($2000/year)
- Commuter benefits (pre-tax)

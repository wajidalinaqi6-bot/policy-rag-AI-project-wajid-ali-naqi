# Code of Conduct

## Professional Behavior
- Treat all colleagues with respect and dignity
- Maintain professional communication
- Respect diversity and inclusion
- No discrimination or harassment of any kind
- Report concerns to HR immediately

## Workplace Safety
- Report all accidents and injuries within 24 hours
- Follow all safety protocols
- Keep work areas clean and organized
- Report unsafe conditions to facilities
- Emergency exits clearly marked

## Conflict of Interest
- Disclose any potential conflicts to manager
- Cannot accept gifts from vendors over $50
- Cannot work for competitors without approval
- Must recuse from decisions affecting family members
- Outside employment requires written approval

## Confidentiality
- Protect company proprietary information
- Do not discuss salary or performance with peers
- Customer data must remain confidential
- Non-disclosure agreements must be signed
- Obligations continue after employment ends

## Intellectual Property
- All work created during employment belongs to company
- Must assign patent rights to company
- Respect third-party intellectual property
- Open source contributions require approval

## Reporting Violations
- Report to: hr@company.com or ethics hotline
- No retaliation for good-faith reports
- Investigation conducted promptly
- Confidentiality maintained throughout
